      UV values come from a clear-sky model based on sun angle and the ozone overhead, both taken from Open-Meteo's UV forecast for the day (ozone is worked out from its clear-sky UV, with a seasonal estimate as fallback), then scaled hour by hour for clouds. Real UV also changes with haze, altitude and reflective surfaces like snow, sand or water. UVA and UVB rise are the moments the sun passes the angles set in Settings.
